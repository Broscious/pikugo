    How it works

    Align

    Each photo is shifted so the focus points you picked land on the same pixel. That point stays still while everything else wiggles around it.

    Frame

    Crop to the area every photo shares, or layer them and keep all of it. Then pick an aspect ratio and decide where the subject sits.

    Encode

    A background worker draws each frame on an OffscreenCanvas, compresses it with your browser's WebP encoder, and stitches the frames into one animated file.

    Pikugo runs entirely in your browser. Your photos are never uploaded.
